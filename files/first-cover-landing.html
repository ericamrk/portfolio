What changes at 25

Two kinds of cover can end on the same day. Here's what usually happens, and what you can do about it.

  • Often at 25

    Your parent's benefits plan

    Most family plans check that you're a full-time student from 21 and stop covering you at 25 (26 in Quebec). Check the plan's booklet for the exact date.

  • On your 25th birthday

    OHIP+ prescriptions

    Ontario pays for most prescriptions for people 24 and under who have no private drug plan. That stops on your 25th birthday.

  • The same day, if you like

    First Cover

    Prescriptions are included in every plan. Apply within 60 days of losing group coverage and we won't ask any health questions.

Sources: Ontario.ca, OHIP+ · Manulife, student dependant coverage

      Plain words

      The words insurance uses, explained

      Premium
      What you pay each month.
      Deductible
      What you pay before the plan starts paying. Ours is $0.
      Waiting period
      The time between signing up and when a benefit starts. 0 days for prescriptions.
      Co-insurance
      The share of a bill the plan pays. "80%" means you pay $20 of a $100 bill.
      Annual maximum
      The most a module pays in one plan year. It resets every year.
      Group coverage
      Benefits that come through an employer or a parent's employer.
