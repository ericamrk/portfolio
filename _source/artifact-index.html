002(About)

Toronto · English / 中文

I trained in fashion design and work as a graphic designer, so I care about how a product feels as much as how a page reads.

At DKR Apparel I design quarterly wholesale catalogues, email campaigns and production artwork, and keep every touchpoint on brand across design, marketing and operations. Before that I made bilingual campaign graphics and in-store signage for retail and hospitality brands in Toronto and Sanya.

I hold an Honours BA in Fashion Design from Toronto Metropolitan University (GPA 4.0). My thesis collection, Metamorphosis, was selected by Fashion Art Toronto.
